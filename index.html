Caleb Mission for the Homeless

Caleb Mission is a charity non-profit organization founded by Rev. Kwak in 2015. Rooted in 1 Peter 5:10, our mission is to walk with individuals facing hardship as God restores, strengthens, and establishes them. Our organization is teamed with youth and the members of the community who come together with a shared commitment to companionship, encouragement, and community support.

About Us

We walk alongside our most vulnerable neighbors — sharing warm meals with the homeless, companionship with lonely seniors, and care for children and students with special needs. Your gift helps restore, strengthen, and establish joy and grace in our community, together.
