Caleb Mission for the Homeless

Caleb Mission is a charity non-profit organization founded in 2015. Rooted in 1 Peter 5:10, our mission is to walk with individuals facing hardship as God restores, strengthens, and establishes them. Our organization is teamed with youth and the members of the community who come together with a shared commitment to companionship, encouragement, and community support.

About Us

We are a community‑driven nonprofit focused on weekly meal services, outreach programs, and building human connection. Every meal is served with dignity, respect, and compassion.
